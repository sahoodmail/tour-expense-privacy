🧳 Tour Expense Manager

Track and split group travel expenses easily. Create tours, add expenses, settle balances, and securely back up your data to Google Drive.

Features

  • Create tours with multiple members
  • Track expenses by category with smart splitting
  • Debt simplification engine for easy settlements
  • Analytics with pie charts and bar charts
  • Secure Google Drive backup & restore
  • Dark & Light mode support

How We Use Google Data

Tour Expense Manager uses Google Sign-In for authentication and Google Drive's app-specific folder to back up and restore your expense database. We never access any other files on your Google Drive.
